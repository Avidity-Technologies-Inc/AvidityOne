## Automatic flags

Severity values below reproduce the source's proposed defaults. They remain configurable according to the approved program policy. All flags support activation, thresholds/applicability, evidence, version history and audited overrides.

| ID | Source flag and default severity | Dependencies | Required acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RF-01 | Undocumented Close, High | Resolution evidence, valid public response history, close events | Closing without resolution notes or without qualifying client communication creates a required review; unrelated notes/automated acknowledgment do not silently satisfy the rule |
| RF-02 | Reopened Ticket, High | Historical close/reopen cycles | Reopen within the configured 7/14/30-day window flags against the correct earlier close; outside-window and subsequent cycles remain explainable |
| RF-03 | Ownership Churn, Medium | Assignment history and responsibility definition | Transfer across three or more technicians is detectable under the configured threshold; simultaneous collaboration is not automatically counted as churn |
| RF-04 | Stalled Ticket, Medium | Business clock and qualified technical activity | Active work with no qualifying activity for the configured business-hours period is flagged; QC reads/alerts do not reset activity |
| RF-05 | Time Entry Variance, Medium | Time entries, categories and historical baseline | Missing time and material variance are detected under explicit rules; inadequate comparison history is disclosed instead of producing unsupported conclusions |
| RF-06 | Unverified RMM Close, High | Alert-ticket-device correlation and current provider evidence | Alert-origin work closed without the configured corresponding check-in/clearance evidence is flagged; stale/unavailable/unconfigured telemetry is distinguishable from verification |
| RF-07 | Silent Aging, High | Human public communications and open-work scope | Lack of client communication beyond the configured period triggers QC; internal notes and routine system notices do not reset the clock |

## Automatic flag and notification implementation status

All RF-01–RF-07 rules are implemented in `qc.engine.service.ts`, configured in `QcSettings.tsx` and retained as findings with audited overrides. Closure evidence is frozen at close; RMM requires explicit client/device/check correlation and fresh pre-close observation. Insufficient historical labor is reported rather than inferred. All thresholds and severities remain configurable, and all flags start disabled. DB/RT cover selected mandatory cases, late findings, reinspection, scope, unavailable RMM and verified synthetic observation; representative real workload acceptance remains pending for each flag.

NR-01–NR-08 are expressible through persisted event/audience/channel/delay routes; no source suggestion is installed as an active business default. Quiet hours, urgent exceptions, rate limits, digest schedule and acknowledgment stop semantics are settings. The in-app path was exercised against PostgreSQL and concurrent delivery. Teams cards/callbacks and Outlook use real provider adapters, with no live tenant send performed. Each external route still requires actual authorized-recipient, provider-failure and recovery acceptance.

SM-01–SM-05 have the underlying cycle, score, criterion, finding, delivery/acknowledgment and safe-export records. Business improvement cannot be claimed from synthetic tests: establish real baseline/cohorts and targets during M5 and compare subsequent periods. Delivery/acknowledgment timestamps are available for latency analysis; no improvement target is invented.
